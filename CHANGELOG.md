## [1.2.0] - 2026-10-27

### Added

- Cookie policy, listing every cookie and bit of browser storage the site uses.
- You choose whether external media may load: YouTube videos and images or sounds from other sites wait until you
  allow them, for one clue or for good. Change your mind any time with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of the page.
- Hosts can delete a game straight away, together with everyone's names and scores.
- A privacy link on the phone buzzer.

### Changed

- The privacy notice now also covers the lobby, application logs and backups, and how players can get their name removed.
- The terms say it plainly: the service is free, with no fees and nothing to refund.
- The lobby's banner and background are now made for this site instead of images borrowed from other websites.
- Easier to read and to use with a keyboard: stronger text contrast, a visible focus outline on every field, and a
  sound seek bar that works with the arrow keys. The pulsing play button stays still if you prefer reduced motion.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-10-27

### Added

- Take turns: one team gets a head start on each clue, then anyone can steal.
- Pressing the buzzer too early locks it for a second.
- Privacy notice, terms and legal notice.

### Fixed

- The clue timer no longer freezes or keeps running after Restart.
- Buzzers open after a short random delay, so nobody can time them.
- Open the buzzers from the host page (or press O), out of sight of the stream.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-25

### Added

- Jeopardy is live: build a board, host it, and play with buzzers on your phone.
