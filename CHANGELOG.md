## [1.1.0] - 2026-10-27

### Added

- Take turns: one team gets a head start on each clue, then anyone can steal.
- Pressing the buzzer too early locks it for a second.
- Privacy notice, terms and legal notice.

### Fixed

- The clue timer no longer freezes or keeps running after Restart.
- Buzzers open after a short random delay, so nobody can time them.
- Open the buzzers from the host page (or press O), out of sight of the stream.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-25

### Added

- Jeopardy is live: build a board, host it, and play with buzzers on your phone.
